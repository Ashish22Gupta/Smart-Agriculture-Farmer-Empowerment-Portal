Account & Registration

To register on the portal:

  1. Open the portal and enter your mobile number linked to Aadhaar
  2. Receive a 4-digit OTP via SMS
  3. Enter the OTP to verify your identity
  4. Your account is automatically created using your Aadhaar-linked details

The entire process takes less than 2 minutes and requires no additional documents.

If you don't receive the OTP:

  • Wait for 60 seconds before requesting a new OTP
  • Check if your mobile has proper network coverage
  • Ensure your mobile number is correctly linked to Aadhaar
  • Try the "Resend OTP" button after the timer expires

If the issue persists, contact our helpline at 1800-180-1551.

Upload Crop Bill

The 30-minute window ensures:

  • Authenticity: You're uploading the bill immediately after purchase
  • Location verification: Geolocation confirms you're at the mandi/market
  • Prevents fraud: Reduces chances of duplicate or old bill submissions

If the timer expires, you'll need to wait for your next market visit to upload a new bill.

We accept the following bill types:

  • MSP Receipt: Official receipt from government procurement centers
  • Mandi Slip: Computer-generated slip from APMC mandis
  • FPO Receipt: Bills from registered Farmer Producer Organizations
  • Private Buyer Invoice: GST invoice from registered traders

The bill must clearly show: crop type, quantity, price, and seller/buyer details.

Common reasons for bill rejection:

  • Blurry image: Ensure the bill is clearly visible and well-lit
  • Incomplete details: All required fields (crop, quantity, price) must be visible
  • Duplicate submission: The same bill number was already uploaded
  • Invalid format: Only JPG, PNG, or PDF files under 8 MB are accepted

You can retake the photo and try again within the 30-minute window.

Rewards & Vouchers

To redeem your vouchers:

  1. Go to "My Winnings" and click "Show Code" on the voucher
  2. Copy the voucher code (e.g., KRXXXXXX)
  3. Visit your nearest Krishi Seva Kendra or authorized dealer
  4. Show the code and collect your reward (seeds, fertilizer, equipment, etc.)

Vouchers are valid for the duration mentioned on the voucher card.

No, vouchers are non-transferable.

Each voucher is linked to your Farmer ID and can only be redeemed by you. This prevents misuse and ensures benefits reach the intended farmer.

If you're unable to collect the reward yourself, you can authorize a family member by contacting customer support with a written authorization letter.

Government Schemes

You can check PM-KISAN status in two ways:

  1. Online: Visit pmkisan.gov.in → "Beneficiary Status" → Enter Aadhaar/mobile
  2. Portal: The Smart Krishi Portal shows your PM-KISAN benefits in the dashboard

If payment is pending, ensure your Aadhaar is seeded with your bank account and land records are updated.

Technical Support

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  • Check internet: Ensure you have stable 4G/WiFi connection
  • Clear cache: Go to browser settings → Clear browsing data
  • Update browser: Use latest Chrome, Safari, or Firefox
  • Try incognito mode: This rules out extension conflicts

The portal is optimized for low-bandwidth areas and works on 2G networks.
