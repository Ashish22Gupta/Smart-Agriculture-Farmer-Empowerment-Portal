# 🌾 Smart Krishi Portal
### Smart Agriculture & Farmer Empowerment Platform

A comprehensive, responsive web portal designed to empower Indian farmers with government scheme information, digital rewards, educational content, and modern farming resources.

## ✨ Features

### 🧑‍🌾 Farmer Portal (B2C)
- **Secure OTP Login** - Aadhaar-linked mobile verification
- **Dashboard** - Scheme benefits, rewards, and activity overview
- **Crop Bill Upload** - 30-minute timed uploads with geolocation verification
- **Scratch & Win** - Gamified agri-input reward reveal (seeds, fertilizer, equipment)
- **Digital Wallet** - View and redeem earned vouchers at Krishi Kendras
- **Krishi Gyan** - Video tutorials and shorts on modern farming
- **Experience Booking** - Book drone training and smart farm visits
- **Scheme Details** - PM-KISAN, PMFBY, Soil Health Card, KCC, and more
- **FAQs** - Comprehensive help section with search

### 🎨 Three Distinct Themes
1. **Bhoomi (The Earth)** - Warm terracotta & gold (Default farmer theme)
2. **Krishi Tech** - Professional teal (Field officer theme)
3. **Harit (Organic)** - Fresh green (Sustainability theme)

### 📱 Fully Responsive
- Desktop: Multi-column dashboard with persistent sidebar
- Tablet: Adaptive 2-column layouts
- Mobile: Touch-optimized with drawer navigation
