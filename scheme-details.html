PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Direct income support for landholding farmer families

payments
₹6,000 / year Direct bank transfer
calendar_month
3 Installments Every 4 months

check_circle Eligibility

  • All landholding farmer families across India
  • Must have cultivable land recorded in revenue records
  • Small and marginal farmers with up to 2 hectares
  • Excludes institutional landholders and income tax payers

description Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
  • Land ownership documents / Khasra Khatauni
  • Bank account details (linked with Aadhaar)
  • Mobile number (for OTP verification)

format_list_numbered How to Apply

  1. Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or agriculture office
  2. Submit application form with required documents
  3. Get Aadhaar seeding done with bank account
  4. Track status on pmkisan.gov.in using registration number

Comprehensive crop insurance against natural calamities

percent
2% Premium For Kharif crops
umbrella
Full Coverage Until post-harvest

check_circle Eligibility

  • All farmers including sharecroppers and tenant farmers
  • Growing notified crops in notified areas
  • Both loanee and non-loanee farmers eligible

description Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Land records / Tenant agreement
  • Bank account passbook
  • Sowing certificate (for non-loanee farmers)

format_list_numbered How to Apply

  1. Enroll before crop sowing deadline through bank/CSC
  2. Pay premium amount (balance subsidized by government)
  3. Report crop loss within 72 hours of incident
  4. Claim settled directly to bank account after assessment

Affordable credit for agricultural and allied activities

trending_down
4% Interest With timely repayment
credit_score
Up to ₹3 Lakh Without collateral

check_circle Eligibility

  • All farmers - individual or joint
  • Tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers
  • Self Help Groups of farmers

description Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card and PAN (for loans above ₹1.6 lakh)
  • Land records / proof of cultivation
  • Passport size photographs

format_list_numbered How to Apply

  1. Visit your nearest bank branch (public or private)
  2. Submit application with documents
  3. Loan sanction and KCC issued within 14 days
  4. Withdraw funds as needed, repay within crop cycle

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana

Promotion of organic farming through cluster approach

payments
₹50,000 / hectare Over 3 years
groups
50 Farmer Group 50 acre cluster

check_circle Eligibility

  • Farmers willing to adopt organic farming
  • Must form group of 50 farmers with 50 acre area
  • Land should not have used chemical fertilizers recently

description Required Documents

  • Group registration with agriculture department
  • Land records of all group members
  • Aadhaar cards of all farmers
  • Resolution passed by farmer group

format_list_numbered How to Apply

  1. Form group of 50 farmers in your area
  2. Register with District Agriculture Office
  3. Undergo 3-year organic certification process
  4. Receive financial support and training
